LAB 3 - ESP32 FreeRTOS MQTT WEBSITE

1. Trong Wokwi, mo du an da luu. Sao luu sketch.ino cu, sau do dan noi dung esp32_freertos_mqtt.ino vao sketch.ino.
2. Library Manager can co: DHT sensor library for ESPx, PubSubClient.
3. Khong doi mach: DHT22 SDA -> GPIO15; MQ2 AOUT -> GPIO34; LED do 1 (bom) -> GPIO26; LED do 2 (quat) -> GPIO25.
4. Chay Wokwi va cho den khi Serial Monitor hien '[MQTT] CONNECTED', '[MQTT] SENT'.
5. Mo index.html bang Google Chrome (nhan dup vao file). May tinh can ket noi Internet de tai MQTT.js va Chart.js.
6. Trang web phai hien 'MQTT da ket noi'. Theo doi nhiet do va gas sau 2.5 giay.
7. Nhan 'Thu cong', sau do thu bat/tat quat va bom. Serial Monitor se hien '[MQTT CMD]'. Thu chuyen ve 'Tu dong'.
8. Topic goc tren Website: lab3/huubui/fire01. Neu thay doi, phai doi ca 3 TOPIC_* trong sketch.ino cho giong nhau.

Luu y: Broker cong khai, khong bao mat; chi dung de mo phong. Gas ADC khong phai ppm. Manual co the tat thiet bi du moi truong vuot nguong: khong dung thiet ke nay de bao ve chay thuc te.
